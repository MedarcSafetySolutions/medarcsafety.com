Wearable charting for air medical and ground EMS crews

The chart shouldn't wait for the flight to end.

In transport medicine, crews treat first and chart later, rebuilding times and doses from gloves, tape and memory after the patient is gone. That costs crew time, billing accuracy and legal protection.

Why ARCTIN?

For the crew

One tap, exact time.

Vitals, medications, procedures and mission milestones are time-stamped the moment they happen, worn on the arm and usable with gloves on.

Safer medication.

Six-rights verification and a second-crew-member check are built into every medication entry. Every medication is checked for IV compatibility with what is already infusing, and if two don't mix, ARCTIN suggests starting another line.

Prompts that catch what busy crews miss.

Alerts for falling blood pressure and abnormal heart rate, and reminders to reassess pain after analgesia.

The narrative writes itself.

ARCTIN Hub builds the narrative in your program's own format from what was actually charted, ready at handoff.

A chart check before submission

Flags missing items and contradictions.

Works without internet.

ARCTIN Hub runs from the device over its own Wi-Fi, in the aircraft, the ambulance or a remote village.

A breathing break between missions.

Breathe, built into ARCTIN: a short guided breathing session (1 to 5 minutes) with a sand picture that settles as you breathe. For relaxation only; never charted, and not available during critical care.

ARCTIN is a documentation tool. It records and reminds; the crew makes every care decision. ARCTIN does not diagnose, treat, or determine doses. Dose information is reference only and must be verified by the clinician against the program's protocols and the medication label before administration.

No patient names are stored on the device. ARCTIN creates its own WiFi network and does not send data to any outside server. ARCTIN Hub does not save patient data to disk and clears the case automatically after inactivity and after export. A business associate agreement (BAA) is signed before any live patient use.

How it works

From the first tap to a finished chart.

  1. Tap

    The crew taps ARCTIN as care happens: milestones, vitals, medications, procedures and assessments.

  2. Watch it build

    ARCTIN Hub on a tablet or laptop shows the live timeline, vital-sign trends and the narrative as it forms.

  3. Finish the chart

    Chart check points out anything missing. Copy the narrative into your ePCR in your agency's format.

  4. Improve

    Quality-measure reports and research exports turn every flight into program data.
